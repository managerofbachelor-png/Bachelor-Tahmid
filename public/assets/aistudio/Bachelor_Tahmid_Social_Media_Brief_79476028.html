Strengths & gaps

✓  What is working

  • Clear, likeable niche with distinctive “raw / unfiltered” positioning that reads the same everywhere it can be checked.
  • Serious output: 295 videos and a genuinely regular ~2–3-per-week cadence on YouTube — consistency most small creators never reach.
  • Cross-linking habit is already there, plus a link-in-bio hub.
  • Multi-format range: vlog, food, thrift, geopolitics / infrastructure commentary, and gaming livestreams.
  • An early gaming community (“Respawn Tahmid”) with its own handles and a ~500-follower Facebook page.

✕  What is holding it back

  • Views, not volume: most recent YouTube videos sit under 100 views despite 295 uploads; +78 videos added only ~39 subscribers in 7.5 months.
  • TikTok & Facebook are effectively dormant as audiences — 2 and 7 followers — with no visible content strategy there.
  • Half the footprint is unverifiable: X, Rumble, Bilibili and LinkedIn returned no public presence.
  • Split identity: name / handle inconsistencies and the separate gaming brand dilute search and invite confusion or impersonation.
  • No visible monetisation beyond a “PR / Promotions welcome” line on TikTok — no sponsorships or press were found.
